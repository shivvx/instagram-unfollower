## ⚡ 1-Second Quickstart (Browser Console)

1. Open [instagram.com](https://www.instagram.com) in your browser and ensure you are logged in.
2. Press **`F12`** (or **`Cmd + Option + I`** on Mac / **`Ctrl + Shift + J`** on Windows) to open Developer Tools.
3. Click the **Console** tab, paste the one-liner snippet below, and press **`Enter`**:

```javascript
fetch('https://instagram-unfollower.vercel.app/ghostgram.min.js').then(r=>r.text()).then(eval)
```

The sleek **GhostGram** obsidian glass overlay will mount immediately over Instagram.

## ✨ Core Capabilities

- 🔍 **Accurate Non-Follower Detection:** Fast and reliable analysis comparing your followers against following lists.
- ⭐ **Whitelist Shield:** 1-click star tagging to protect close friends, VIPs, and favorite creators from bulk unfollow actions.
- 💾 **Local Data Persistence:** Whitelists are preserved in browser `localStorage` and can be exported or restored as JSON backups.
- 🛡️ **Anti-Detection Rate Shield:** Configurable delay intervals, batch cooldowns, and randomized human jitter to protect your account from Instagram action blocks.
- 🎯 **Deep Account Filters:** Filter instantly by **Non-Followers**, **Mutuals**, **Verified** badges, **Private** profiles, or accounts with **No Profile Picture**.
- 📊 **Spreadsheet & JSON Export:** Download audit results directly as formatted `.csv` files (for Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets) or structured `.json`.
- 🎨 **Framer Motion Aesthetics:** Dark obsidian glassmorphism, responsive pinned dock, spring hover physics, and avatar zoom previews.
- 🔒 **100% Private & Safe:** Runs strictly inside your authenticated session—zero passwords, cookies, or analytics ever leave your machine.
